Twelve commitments.

The non-negotiables. If a design move breaks one of these, it does not ship.

01

The site is a film, not a brochure.

Every section is a scene; the scroll is the cut. Compose the homepage like an edit, not a stack.

02

The cursor is a brand element.

A custom cursor ships on day one. It tells you what is interactive without copy.

03

Motion is purposeful, never decorative.

Reveals hierarchy, signals interactivity, carries emotion — or it does not ship.

04

Restraint is also bold.

Two colors, two type families, one accent. Quiet 80% so we can scream 20%.

05

Performance is craft.

60fps on a Pixel 6. 1.4s LCP. The expensive feel comes from speed.

06

The work is the hero.

Case studies are the product. The home page launches them.

07

Accessibility is the floor.

Keyboard reachable, screen-reader sane, reduced motion respected. No exceptions.

08

Type is the personality.

The brand voice lives in the typeface — and in how it moves.

09

Color is a weapon, not a wardrobe.

One accent does the work of ten. We do not roll out a rainbow.

10

Case interiors are sandboxes.

Chrome stays consistent. Each case interior is allowed its own art direction.

11

Mobile-first, desktop-grand.

Every hero has a designed mobile twin — never a ‘degraded’ one.

12

The site is alive.

Even idle, something breathes — a gradient, a marquee, a slow drift.
